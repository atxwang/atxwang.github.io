As the year closes, I thought I'd make a list of songs, albums, performances etc. that I especially enjoyed this year- in particular, to highlight music that I haven't seen in many critics' year-end lists (I've been cross-referencing Pitchfork to make sure we don't have too much overlap)! So while I like Geese Wednesday Cameron Winter MJ Lenderman This is Lorelei etc etc etc as much as everyone else, they won't be mentioned here. Without further ado:

**Nina Protocol's Instagram presence**
-
[Nina Protocol](https://www.dazeddigital.com/music/article/63054/1/music-streaming-platform-of-the-future-nina-protocol) is a new streaming platform that aims to give independent artists more direct control over their product and earnings using blockchain. Regardless of your feelings on Web3, streaming, or the independent music industry, it cannot be denied that Nina has a great social media presence, featuring both great recommendations of new music and interviews with artists like Yung Lean, Mac DeMarco, and Bassvictim. Nina's put me onto two of my favorite projects this year: screamo band Crochet's *Cherish* and post-rock outfit punxsutawney's debut EP.
